# ⚠️ Data Limitations & Considerations

## 📌 Overview

This document outlines the limitations of the dataset and analysis. Understanding these constraints is essential for accurate interpretation of results and for identifying areas of improvement.

---

## 🔹 1. Data Availability Constraints

* The dataset includes **sales data only**
* No **profit or cost-related information** is available
* Limits the ability to perform profitability analysis

---

## 🔹 2. Limited Feature Scope

* Dataset lacks advanced business attributes such as:

  * Customer lifetime value
  * Product cost structure
  * Marketing or campaign data

* Restricts deeper business insights

---

## 🔹 3. Time Context Limitations

* Historical data is available, but:

  * No external context (seasonality drivers, events)
  * No comparison with industry benchmarks

---

## 🔹 4. Analytical Constraints

* Analysis is limited to:

  * Sales trends
  * Customer contribution
  * Regional and category performance

* Advanced analytics (predictive modeling, profitability) not fully possible

---

## 🔹 5. Data Granularity

* Data is at transaction level but:

  * No detailed breakdown of operational costs
  * Limited scope for margin analysis

---

## 🔹 6. External Factors Not Included

The dataset does not account for:

* Market conditions
* Competitor activity
* Economic factors

These may influence sales but are not reflected in the data

---

## 💡 Impact on Analysis

* Insights are **sales-focused** rather than profitability-focused
* Strategic recommendations are limited to available data scope
* Results should be interpreted with these constraints in mind

---

## 🚀 Future Improvements

To enhance analysis:

* Integrate profit and cost data
* Include customer lifecycle metrics
* Add external datasets (market trends, seasonality factors)
* Apply advanced analytics and forecasting models

---

## 🎯 Conclusion

While the dataset provides valuable insights into sales performance, understanding its limitations ensures responsible and accurate interpretation of results and highlights opportunities for future enhancement.
