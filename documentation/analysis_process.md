# 📊 Analysis Process

## 📌 Objective

The objective of this analysis is to extract actionable insights from sales data to support business decision-making and identify growth opportunities.

---

## 🔄 End-to-End Workflow

The analysis followed a structured workflow:

**Excel → SQL → Power BI**

* Excel: Initial data inspection and validation
* SQL: Data querying and aggregation
* Power BI: Data visualization and dashboard creation

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Initial exploration was performed to understand:

* Data distribution and patterns
* Trends over time
* Category-wise and region-wise performance

Key steps:

* Analyzed sales distribution
* Identified top-performing segments
* Detected anomalies and inconsistencies

---

## 🗄 SQL-Based Analysis

SQL was used to perform structured analysis on the cleaned dataset.

### Key operations:

* Aggregation of sales by region, category, and customer
* Identification of top-performing products and segments
* Ranking and filtering for business insights

### Example focus areas:

* Top customers by sales
* Region-wise sales performance
* Category contribution to total sales

---

## 📊 Business-Oriented Analysis

The analysis was aligned with business questions:

* Which regions generate the highest sales?
* Which categories drive the most revenue?
* Who are the most valuable customers?
* What are the sales trends over time?

---

## 📈 Key Focus Areas

* Sales trends (time-based analysis)
* Regional performance comparison
* Customer contribution analysis
* Product/category performance

---

## 🎯 Outcome

The analysis enabled:

* Identification of high-performing areas
* Recognition of underperforming segments
* Better understanding of customer behavior
* Data-driven decision support

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

* No profit or cost data available
* Analysis limited to sales-based metrics
* External influencing factors not included
