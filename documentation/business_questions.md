# ❓ Business Questions

## 📌 Overview

This document outlines the key business questions addressed through the analysis and dashboard. These questions guide the analytical approach and ensure that the insights generated are aligned with business objectives.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To use data-driven analysis to answer critical business questions related to sales performance, customer behavior, and market trends.

---

## 🔹 1. Sales Performance Analysis

### ❓ Key Questions

* What is the overall sales performance of the business?
* How do sales vary over time (monthly/yearly trends)?
* Are there any noticeable patterns or seasonality in sales?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To evaluate overall business growth and identify trends that impact revenue.

---

## 🔹 2. Regional Performance

### ❓ Key Questions

* Which regions generate the highest sales?
* Which regions are underperforming?
* How does sales distribution vary geographically?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To support regional strategy, resource allocation, and expansion planning.

---

## 🔹 3. Customer Behavior Analysis

### ❓ Key Questions

* Who are the top customers contributing to total sales?
* How is revenue distributed across customers?
* Are there customers who contribute disproportionately to sales?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To identify high-value customers and improve customer retention strategies.

---

## 🔹 4. Product & Category Performance

### ❓ Key Questions

* Which product categories generate the most sales?
* Which products are top-selling?
* Are there categories with low performance?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To optimize product strategy, inventory planning, and marketing focus.

---

## 🔹 5. Sales Trends & Patterns

### ❓ Key Questions

* How does sales performance change over time?
* Are there any recurring trends or fluctuations?
* What insights can be derived from historical data?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To understand demand patterns and improve forecasting accuracy.

---

## 🔹 6. Strategic Decision-Making

### ❓ Key Questions

* Where should the business focus to increase sales?
* Which areas require improvement?
* What opportunities exist for growth?

### 🎯 Business Purpose

To support data-driven decision-making and business strategy development.

---

## 📊 Mapping Questions to Dashboard

| Business Area     | Dashboard Page    | Insights Delivered           |
| ----------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Sales Performance | Sales Overview    | KPIs, trends, category sales |
| Customer Analysis | Customer Analysis | Top customers, distribution  |
| Forecasting       | Sales Forecast    | Future trends                |

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway

The business questions act as the foundation of the entire analysis process. By aligning data exploration with these questions, the project ensures that all insights are meaningful, actionable, and relevant to real-world business scenarios.
